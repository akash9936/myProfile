What I Work On

Lender Integrations

I connect a lending platform to NBFC lending partners: loan booking, disbursal, co-lending arrangements and the status callbacks that keep both sides in agreement about every loan.

Loan Servicing

Repayment schedules, EMI payment marking, part-payments, pre-closure and settlement. This is the part of lending where a wrong number becomes a customer complaint, so correctness and reconciliation come first.

Onboarding & KYC

Video KYC and document verification flows, and the operational tooling that lets an ops team process applications without an engineer in the loop.

Service Migration

Moving a production integration service onto a new codebase, one lender and one flow at a time, without interrupting live disbursals.

Production Support

Root-cause analysis from logs and data, runbooks for recurring issues, and staged releases across multiple services, verified pod by pod.

AI-Assisted Engineering

I build agent harnesses around LLMs: scheduled loops, enumerated permissions, checkpointed state, and held-out evals that measure whether a prompt change helped.
